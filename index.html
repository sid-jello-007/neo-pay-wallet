The thinking

Three products, one platform, regulated from day one

Neo wanted its own payments layer: a mobile wallet residents and visitors use every day, a business app local merchants use to get paid, and back office tools for operations, compliance and support. The work moved through three layers, each feeding the next.

1. Map every capability→2. Sequence by outcome→3. Design the journeys

Three products, not one

Customer app, merchant app and back office each have their own outcomes. If operations can't reconcile payments, letting merchants accept them is pointless.

Regulation from day one

Digital KYC, KYB, sanctions screening, risk based limits and fraud rules had to be in the first release, not bolted on later.

A city that doesn't exist yet

Few merchants, patchy connectivity on sites still under construction, and a population that changes every month. Offline payments and fast onboarding mattered more than usual.

Decisions and lessons

What shaped the product

DecisionWhy
Outcome based roadmap across three user groupsKept back office readiness in lockstep with app features
Risk score drives limits, not a pass/fail gateUsers get in fast with lower limits; verification unlocks more
Identity proven via the OTP linked to the national IDProves the person owns the identity without a branch visit or document upload
Offline QR payments in the second incrementConnectivity on a city under construction couldn't be assumed
Save and resume for merchant applicationsKYB needs documents most owners don't have to hand
Maker / checker for compliance exceptionsAuditability that risk and compliance required
Cashier roles and a static QR per cashierMatches how real shops run tills and shifts

Back office is the critical path

Reconciliation, settlement and exception handling decide the launch date, not the app.

Compliance is a design partner

Bringing them into journey design early turned "no" into "yes, with these limits".

Sequence by risk, not excitement

Split bills and offers are fun; limits, fraud rules and refunds make a wallet safe to scale.
